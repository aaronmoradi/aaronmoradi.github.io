# Check In changelog

Bugs and support questions are tracked as GitHub issues labelled `checkin`. Each release below lists what was fixed.

## 0.4.0 · Oct 25, 2026 · First iPhone test build
- Installable from Safari (Share › Add to Home Screen), works offline after the first visit.
- "Use my current location" uses the phone's real location, matched to a city on the device. Coordinates are never sent anywhere.
- Check-ins, trips and settings are saved on the phone between launches.
- Help & feedback screen: report a bug, ask a question, see what's new, see your reports.
- Sample dates move with today's date.
- Fixed: the city sheet had no way to close it on a phone.

## 0.3.0 · Oct 19, 2026 · Privacy, flights and overlaps
- Current city instead of home city, with the option to hide it.
- Send delays, never-notify list, daily or weekly summaries, pauses.
- Flight lookup (sample data), city search, overlap alerts, lists, availability.
- Fixed: friend rows said "In town · In Lisbon now".
- Fixed: daily summary showed the wrong dates.

## 0.2.0 · Oct 17, 2026 · Their side
- The notification and check-in screen from a friend's phone.
- Fixed: date fields were cut off on narrow screens.
- Fixed: the confirmation message blocked taps underneath it.

## 0.1.0 · Oct 17, 2026 · Prototype
- Check in, skip friends, plan a trip, see friends around you.
